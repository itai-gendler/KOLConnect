# Legacy Cooperation

A historical cooperation record disclosed as read-only history. Its retained price has no established currency unit.

## Information kept

- **Historical identity** — Addressed historical cooperation identity.
- **Creator association** — Canonical profile whose history contains it.
- **Campaign description** — Historical campaign description.
- **Platform** — Historical platform description.
- **Contact date** — Historical contact date.
- **Untyped price** — Retained historical price without an established currency unit.
- **Publication count** — Historical publication count.
- **Recorded views** — Historical recorded views.
- **Recorded ROI** — Historical recorded ROI.
- **Result** — Historical result description.
- **Notes** — Historical notes.
- **Creation time** — Historical record creation time.
